# 1–2 Minute Demo Script

Use synthetic resumes only.

1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5000` and show the dashboard disclaimer.
2. Paste a sample JD requiring Python, SQL, FastAPI, and three years of experience.
3. Upload two or three synthetic PDF resumes.
4. Click **Process resumes** and show parsed status, matched skills, and missing skills.
5. Explain the ranked score and component evidence; state that it is not a hiring probability.
6. Open a candidate detail API link and show the score breakdown and warnings.
7. Use **Shortlist**, **Reject**, or **Needs review** and enter reviewer notes when required.
8. Export the results to CSV.
9. Briefly show `reports/evaluation_report.md` and `reports/fairness_report.md`, emphasizing their small synthetic scope and limitations.

No video is included; this is a live-demo script.
